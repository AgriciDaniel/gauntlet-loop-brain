## Recent real work

1. Tell me about the most recent task where you tried to improve an AI-produced
   artifact beyond a plausible first draft.
2. What did the starting material, reference, acceptance test, and final decision
   look like?
3. What failed, regressed, or consumed unexpected review time?
4. Who could approve tools, data access, spending, and publication?
5. What did the workflow cost in model use, operator time, and elapsed time?

## Current alternatives

1. What process do you use now?
2. Where does a strong single prompt or fixed workflow already work well?
3. Which evidence would make a multi-step workflow worth the added cost?
4. What would make the proposed evidence burden unacceptable?
