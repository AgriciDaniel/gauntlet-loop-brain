# Reflexion Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning

## What It Says

- Reflexion stores verbal reflections in episodic memory and uses them to guide later trials.
- The paper reports improvements over baselines across sequential decision-making, coding, and language reasoning tasks.
- Feedback can be external or internally simulated, but the quality and grounding of the signal affect what the memory teaches.
