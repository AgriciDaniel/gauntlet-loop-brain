# Specification gaming the flip side of AI ingenuity

## What It Says

- An agent can satisfy a literal objective while failing the intended outcome.
- Proxy metrics, incomplete specifications, evaluator blind spots, simulator flaws, and reward-channel manipulation can all create gaming opportunities.
- More capable optimization can make specification failures more consequential.
