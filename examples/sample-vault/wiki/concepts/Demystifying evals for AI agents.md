# Demystifying evals for AI agents

## What It Says

- Agent evaluation should distinguish transcripts from actual end-state outcomes.
- Code-based, model-based, and human graders have different strengths and should be selected by task.
- Model graders require calibration with human graders, and regression suites are needed to detect backsliding.
- Multiple trials are needed because agent behavior varies between runs.
