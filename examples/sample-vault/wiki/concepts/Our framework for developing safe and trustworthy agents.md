# Our framework for developing safe and trustworthy agents

## What It Says

- Humans should retain control over how agent goals are pursued, especially before high-stakes decisions.
- Anthropic describes read-only defaults, explicit approval for mutations, stop controls, transparency, and layered defenses as parts of trustworthy agent operation.
