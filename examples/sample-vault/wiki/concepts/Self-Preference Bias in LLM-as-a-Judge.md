# Self-Preference Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge

## What It Says

- The study measured significant self-preference bias in GPT-4 under its experimental setup.
- Lower-perplexity, more familiar text received higher LLM evaluations than human evaluations, even beyond literal self-generation.
