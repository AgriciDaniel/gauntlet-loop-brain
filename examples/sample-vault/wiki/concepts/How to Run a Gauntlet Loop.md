# How to Run a Gauntlet Loop

## What It Says

- The named method uses a lead agent, a concrete inspectable bar, decomposed pieces, separate fresh-context critics, direct inspection of the artifact, and repeated refinement.
- The author explicitly says the game did not become better than Call of Duty and that the human stopped the run.
- The author describes one initiating prompt followed by many hours and many subagents, not one model completion.
- The author recommends a whole-artifact smoothing pass after waves of separate changes.
