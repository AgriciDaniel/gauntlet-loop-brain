# Claude of Duty README and honest assessment

## What It Says

- The repository reports roughly 55,000 lines across 11 subsystems and describes its capture, baseline, image-diff, profiling, and playtest tools.
- The stated goal was to match a modern Call of Duty, and the repository says it did not.
- Critic scores were 3.59, 4.14, 4.05, and 5.05 out of 10, so progress was non-monotonic.
- Every critic in every blind comparison round selected the real Call of Duty frame.
- For coupled lighting concerns, three six-agent directory-owned rounds left defects at 60, 47, and 66, while a sequential single-owner pass cut defects from 66 to 26 and moved the score by 1.00.
