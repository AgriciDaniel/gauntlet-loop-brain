## What It Says

- The original prompt asks for a concrete Call of Duty comparison, subagent fan-out, a separate harsh visual critic, blind side-by-side comparison, and repeated looping.
- The original prompt contains aspirational quality language but does not itself prove that the output met that quality bar.
