# 015. Towards a science of scaling agent systems When and why agent systems work

Ledger: 015 | source: Towards a science of scaling agent systems When and why agent systems work | confidence: evidence-based | fold: [[Towards a science of scaling agent systems When and why agent systems work]] | status: captured.

## What It Says

- Google Research reports a controlled evaluation of 180 agent configurations across five architectures, three model families, and four benchmarks.
- Centralized coordination improved the parallelizable Finance-Agent task by 80.9 percent over a single agent, while every tested multi-agent variant degraded sequential PlanCraft performance by 39 to 70 percent.
- Independent multi-agent systems amplified errors by 17.2 times in the reported evaluation, compared with 4.4 times for centralized systems.
