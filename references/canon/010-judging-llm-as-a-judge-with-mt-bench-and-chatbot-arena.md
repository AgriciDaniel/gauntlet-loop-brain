# 010. Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena

Ledger: 010 | source: Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena | confidence: evidence-based | fold: [[Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena]] | status: captured.

## What It Says

- LLM judges can perform pairwise, single-answer, and reference-guided grading.
- The study found position, verbosity, self-enhancement, and reasoning limitations in LLM judges.
- Position swaps, stronger references, complete context, and human agreement checks can mitigate some judge errors.
- No single benchmark determines model quality, so comprehensive evaluation is needed.
