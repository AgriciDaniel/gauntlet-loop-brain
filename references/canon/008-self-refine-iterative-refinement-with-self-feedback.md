# 008. Self-Refine Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback

Ledger: 008 | source: Self-Refine Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback | confidence: evidence-based | fold: [[Self-Refine Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback]] | status: captured.

## What It Says

- Across seven evaluated tasks, iterative same-model feedback and refinement improved average task performance by about 20 percentage points over one-step generation in the reported setup.
- The study capped runs at four feedback-refine iterations and used task-specific stop conditions.
- Specific, actionable feedback mattered, while external oracle feedback produced larger gains in one math setting.
- Refinement could ignore feedback or introduce new problems, so improvement was not guaranteed per trial.
