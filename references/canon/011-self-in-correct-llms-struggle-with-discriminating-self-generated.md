# 011. SELF-[IN]CORRECT LLMs Struggle with Discriminating Self-Generated Responses

Ledger: 011 | source: SELF-[IN]CORRECT LLMs Struggle with Discriminating Self-Generated Responses | confidence: evidence-based | fold: [[SELF-[IN]CORRECT LLMs Struggle with Discriminating Self-Generated Responses]] | status: captured.

## What It Says

- Across the studied models and tasks, models were not reliably better at discriminating among their generated alternatives than at generating initial responses.
- Self-refinement cannot be assumed to improve results solely because the same model judges its own candidates.
